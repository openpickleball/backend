# Backlog changes

Every change applied from `docs/backlog-plan.md`, in order. Approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-12.

## Batch 1: labels

- Created `type:feature`: New behavior or capability
- Created `type:chore`: Tooling, infrastructure, or maintenance
- Created `type:spike`: Decision, investigation, or ADR
- Created `type:epic`: Parent issue that tracks sub-issues
- Renamed `documentation` to `type:docs`: Documentation only
- Renamed `api` to `area:api`: django-ninja endpoints and schema
- Renamed `auth` to `area:auth`: First-party accounts, login, and MFA (allauth)
- Renamed `email` to `area:email`: Transactional email
- Renamed `ratings` to `area:ratings`: Rating state and engine integration
- Renamed `security` to `area:security`: Security-sensitive work
- Renamed `partners` to `area:partners`: Partner OAuth2, partner API, webhooks, and embeds
- Renamed `devops` to `area:infra`: CI, tooling, deployment, and infrastructure
- Created `area:matches`: Matches, the ledger, validation, and uploads
- Created `area:players`: Players, clubs, organizers, events, and privacy
- Created `priority:p0`: On the MVP critical path or an external blocker of it
- Created `priority:p1`: Needed for its milestone, not on the critical path
- Created `priority:p2`: Later milestone
- Created `size:S`: About a day or less
- Created `size:M`: A few days
- Created `size:L`: A week or more; consider splitting
- Renamed `blocked` to `status:blocked`: Waiting on an external decision or dependency
- Kept: `backend`, `data`, `oauth`, `ci`, `testing`, `webhooks` until batch 4 relabels their issues, then deleted.

## Batch 2: milestones

- Milestone 1: renamed `M0 Foundation` to `v0.1 MVP: league results and public ratings`. Description: Goal: a verified organizer uploads league results as CSV in production, and players see their demonstration-labeled ratings. Done when: an organizer upload works in production, ratings update, public rating lookup works, players can sign up, set privacy, dispute, and export, the v0.1 security issues are closed, and a restore drill is recorded.
- Milestone 5: renamed `M4 Partner integration` to `v0.2 Partner API`. Description: Goal: approved platforms submit and read matches over OAuth2. Done when: a sandbox platform registers, gets a client credentials token, submits single and bulk matches, and reads ratings in batch by following only the partner guide, and the legal review outcome is recorded.
- Milestone 2: renamed `M1 Core data model and API` to `v0.3 Rating widget, account linking, and webhooks`. Description: Goal: platforms show live OPEN ratings, link players with Log in with OPEN, and receive rating.updated without polling. Done when: the acceptance criteria of epic #95 are met.
- Milestone 3: renamed `M2 Ratings pipeline` to `v0.4 Player stats, notices, and rating seeds`. Description: Goal: players get stats and rating notices, and can bring a self-reported external seed. Done when: every v0.4 issue is closed, and seeds ship only after the legal review (#81) records its outcome.
- Milestone 4 (`M3 DevOps on DigitalOcean App Platform`): kept until batch 4 moves its issues, then deleted.
